Wildlife Poaching Hotspot Predictor

Environmental Conservation through Data & AI

Project Description

This project uses historical poaching incident records combined with geospatial features such as proximity to roads, water sources, park boundaries, vegetation density, and seasonal patterns to predict which grid cells within a protected area are at elevated poaching risk in the coming month.

Domain

Environmental Conservation

Expected Outcomes

  • Model with precision and recall metrics
  • Risk heatmap visualization overlaid on the protected area
  • Recommended patrol prioritization strategy derived from model output
